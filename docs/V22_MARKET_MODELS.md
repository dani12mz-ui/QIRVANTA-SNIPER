# V2.2 market regime and entry models

The strategy and Intelligence indicator build from one Pine market fragment. The indicator exposes the causal observations and raw candidate geometry. The strategy applies risk and execution policy after confirmation.

The model uses standard OANDA:XAUUSD candles on M5, M15 and M30 after 250 preceding bars. Strict two-left and two-right swing tests require the center price to exceed all four neighbors for a high, or fall below all four for a low. The swing is disclosed at the second right-hand close. Models start using it on the next chart bar.

Local directional bias requires the close, EMA21 and EMA55 in order, both EMAs sloping in the same direction over three bars, and either an aligned HH/HL or LH/LL pivot pair or ADX(14) of at least 18 with the directional index on that side. A BOS is a close across the preceding confirmed swing. An MSS is a BOS opposite the latest BOS direction. The previous completed H1 and H4 candles provide independent close/EMA21/EMA55 context. The Pine request offsets every value and checks that its close timestamp is at or before the chart bar open. [TradingView higher-timeframe behavior](https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/faq/other-data-and-timeframes/)

| Priority | Regime | Reason code |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Exhaustion or overextension | `EMA_DISTANCE` when the close is over 2.50 prior ATR from EMA21, or `WICK_AND_DECELERATION` when an extended prior impulse weakens and the opposing wick spans at least half the range. |
| 2 | Potential reversal | `OPPOSITE_STRUCTURE_BREAK` at an MSS. |
| 3 | Volatility expansion | `TRUE_RANGE_EXPANSION` when current true range reaches 1.50 prior ATR. |
| 4 | Compression | `NARROW_BOX` for the four-bar compression rule, or `ATR_CONTRACTION` when prior ATR is at most 80% of its prior 20-bar mean. |
| 5 | Bullish or bearish trend | `EMA_DIRECTION_WITH_STRUCTURE_OR_DMI` when the local trend qualifies. |
| 6 | Sideways or range | `NO_QUALIFIED_TREND` otherwise. |

The regime classifier records its timestamp and reason whenever the classification changes. ATR ratios use the previous closed bar's ATR14. A candle's directional displacement requires a body of at least 0.80 prior ATR, at least 55% of the bar range, and a close in the final directional quarter.

| Model | Watch | Later confirmation | Structural invalidation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · Structural breakout | Aligned trend, a known swing on both sides, and a close within 0.60 ATR before the break, or an eligible break on this close. | Close strictly crosses the frozen swing in the trend direction. An option adds the displacement requirement. A direct break can emit watch and confirmation together. | Opposite trend, replacement of the frozen swing, an unqualified cross, or expiry after eight bars. The opposite confirmed swing anchors the stop. |
| B · Smart pullback | The prior trend qualifies and price revisits a band 0.20 prior ATR around the prior bar's EMA21 after approaching from the trend side. Freeze the EMA, ATR and touch candle's directional extreme. | A later directional candle closes through the frozen touch extreme with renewed trend alignment. | Opposite trend, a close more than 0.75 frozen ATR beyond the band center against the setup, or expiry after six bars. The most adverse price from touch through confirmation anchors the stop. |
| C · Consolidation continuation | The four-bar high-to-low span is at most 1.50 prior ATR and mean true range at most 0.80 prior ATR. The latest low is higher than the first low for BUY, or the latest high is lower for SELL. The local trend must align. | A later close breaks the frozen box in trend direction with displacement. | Opposite trend, opposite box break, an unqualified directional breakout, or expiry after four bars. The opposite box edge anchors the stop. |

Each model has one watch slot. Its next eligible closed bar emits `CONFIRMING`. A confirmation consumes the watch and starts a six-bar cooldown. The candidate includes the confirmation close, prior ATR, structural stop base, nearest previously disclosed barrier beyond the close, distance from EMA21, and flags for a true-range spike above 2.50 ATR and opposing H1 or H4 context. When neither known swing extends beyond the close, the candidate records the barrier as missing. Strategy fills and management use the strategy's separate audit.

Build the full indicator from the repository root with `python unified/tools/build_v22_intelligence.py`. Add `--check` to compare the generated bytes against the checked-in Pine file. The indicator defaults to 3,600 calculated bars. Its H1 and H4 rows show `WARMUP` until the completed timestamp, close and both EMAs are available. For reproducible TradingView exports, enable **Export observations to Pine Logs** and **Include every OHLC / HTF row**. Set an audit start before the first processed bar and an exact cutoff on a completed chart close.
